About Dulo Akim Foundation

Driving sustainable youth and community development through structured programs and long-term social investment initiatives

Home About Us

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable youth and transform communities through sports development, education support, leadership training, environmental conservation, and economic empowerment across Homa Bay County and beyond.

Our Vision

A thriving community where every young person has access to opportunities for growth, development, and success through structured youth empowerment programs and sustainable social investments.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Community, Empowerment, Innovation, and Sustainability drive everything we do. We believe in creating lasting impact through collaboration and dedication.

Our Story

Who We Are

At Dulo Akim Foundation, we believe potential is universal, but opportunity is not. Our mission is to bridge that gap in Homa Bay County, creating real pathways to success for young people.

We are a catalyst for change, working on the ground to unlock the immense talent within our communities. By integrating sports with education, leadership, and technology, we provide a holistic platform for youth to grow as athletes, students, and future leaders.

From the discipline of the football pitch with Osasa FC to the innovation of our digital literacy workshops, every program is an investment in the next generation. We don't just support youth; we empower them to build a better future for themselves, their families, and their community.

What We Do

Our Focus Areas

Five key pillars driving our mission to empower youth and transform communities

Sports Development & Talent Nurturing

We identify and nurture sporting talent among youth through structured programs, professional coaching, and competitive opportunities. Our Osasa Football Club serves as a platform for youth to develop their athletic abilities while learning valuable life skills.

  • Professional coaching and training
  • Annual tournaments and competitions
  • Talent scouting and development
  • Sports equipment provision

Education Support & Scholarships

Education is the foundation of sustainable development. We provide scholarships, educational materials, and support programs to ensure that talented youth can access quality education regardless of their economic background.

  • Merit-based scholarships
  • Educational materials support
  • Tutoring and mentorship programs
  • School infrastructure improvements

Leadership & Life Skills Empowerment

We equip young people with essential leadership skills, critical thinking abilities, and life competencies that prepare them for success in all areas of life. Our mentorship programs connect youth with experienced professionals.

  • Leadership training workshops
  • Mentorship programs
  • Career guidance and counseling
  • Life skills development

Environmental Conservation & Climate Action

We engage youth in environmental stewardship through tree planting, waste management, and climate awareness programs. Our greening initiatives create sustainable environmental practices while providing learning opportunities.

  • Tree planting campaigns
  • Environmental education
  • Waste management projects
  • Climate change awareness

Economic Empowerment & Mentorship

We create pathways to economic independence through skills training, entrepreneurship programs, and microenterprise development. Our ICT and digital literacy programs prepare youth for the modern economy.

  • ICT & digital literacy training
  • Entrepreneurship programs
  • Vocational skills training
  • Business mentorship
Our Methodology

Our Approach to Impact

1. Identify

We identify vulnerable youth and assess community needs through comprehensive research and grassroots engagement.

2. Engage

We create structured programs that engage youth through sports, education, and skills training opportunities.

3. Develop

We provide continuous support, mentorship, and resources to develop talents and build essential competencies.

4. Empower

We empower youth to become self-reliant, community leaders, and agents of positive change.
