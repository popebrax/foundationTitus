# Landing Page Redesign: Classic Meets Modern

## Overview
Your landing page has been redesigned with a **classic-meets-modern** aesthetic that blends traditional design elements with contemporary features.

---

## Key Design Changes

### 1. **Stats Section Enhancement**
The impact statistics section now features three distinct card styles:

#### Featured Card (Large - Left Side)
- Clean rectangular design with a golden gradient top border
- Prominent icon with subtle rotation on hover
- Animated progress bar showing impact progress
- Modern glass morphism effect with controlled blur
- Smooth elevation effect on hover
- Accent radial gradient background for modern flair

#### Classic Cards (Top Right)
- Traditional rectangular shape with flat, elegant design
- Golden top accent line (classic element)
- Centered icons and typography
- Subtle hover animations (lift + shadow)
- High contrast text for readability

#### Horizontal Card (Bottom)
- Icon on left, stats on right (traditional layout)
- Golden left border accent (classic touch)
- Smooth horizontal slide animation on hover
- Balanced spacing and proportions
