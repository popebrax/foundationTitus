About the Club

More Than Just Football

Osasa Football Club is not just a sports team—it's a community development platform. As one of the major investments of Dulo Akim Foundation, we use football as a tool to identify, nurture, and develop young talent while building character, discipline, and life skills.

Through structured training programs, professional coaching, and competitive opportunities, we provide young people with a pathway to excellence both on and off the field. Our approach combines athletic development with mentorship, education support, and community service.

What We Offer

Programs & Activities

Comprehensive development programs for youth at all skill levels

Youth Academy

Structured football training program for youth aged 12-18, focusing on skill development, tactical awareness, and physical conditioning.

  • Professional coaching
  • Age-appropriate training
  • Skill development focus
  • Regular assessments

Senior Team

Competitive senior squad participating in regional leagues and tournaments, providing pathway to professional football.

  • League competitions
  • Tournament participation
  • Talent showcase events
  • Scout networking

Talent Scouting

Systematic identification and recruitment of talented young players through community tournaments and trials.

  • Annual talent trials
  • Community tournaments
  • Performance tracking
  • Scholarship opportunities

Coaching Development

Training and certification programs for local coaches to build sustainable football development capacity.

  • Coaching courses
  • Mentorship programs
  • Certification support
  • Continuous learning

Sports Science & Wellness

Holistic player development including fitness training, nutrition guidance, and injury prevention programs.

  • Fitness assessments
  • Nutrition education
  • Injury prevention
  • Mental wellness support

Community Outreach

Using football to give back to the community through social programs, charity matches, and youth clinics.

  • Charity matches
  • School programs
  • Community clinics
  • Social campaigns
