About the Program

Transforming Lives Through Sports

The Osasa Youth Empowerment through Sports (O-YES) Initiative is the flagship project of Dulo Akim Foundation, implemented through Osasa Football Club. This comprehensive program goes beyond traditional sports development to create holistic youth empowerment opportunities.

With a three-year strategic framework and a budget of KES 9.84 million, O-YES reaches more than 500 young people annually in Kibiri Ward and surrounding communities, providing them with skills, opportunities, and support systems that transform their lives.

Program Goal

To empower young people in Kibiri Ward through sports, education, leadership, environmental stewardship, and economic opportunities under the Dulo Akim Foundation.

Our Targets

Specific Objectives

01

Talent Development

Identify and nurture sporting talent among youth aged 12–26 through structured training, coaching, and competitive opportunities.

02

Mentorship & Training

Provide mentorship, leadership, and life-skills training to more than 500 youth annually through workshops and programs.

03

Behavior Change

Promote positive behavior change, discipline, teamwork, and community service among participating youth.

04

Economic Empowerment

Establish youth-friendly economic empowerment opportunities linked to sports, ICT, and agribusiness.

05

Environmental Action

Implement greening and environmental conservation programs driven by youth participants.

06

Gender Equality

Promote gender equality and inclusion in all program activities and opportunities.

What We Do

Key Program Activities

Comprehensive activities designed to develop well-rounded, empowered youth

Football Tournaments & Scouting

Annual competitive tournaments and systematic talent scouting programs to identify and develop promising athletes.

Youth Football Academy

Establishment and operation of Osasa Youth Football Academy with professional coaching and training facilities.

Leadership Workshops

Regular mentorship and leadership training workshops to develop critical thinking and decision-making skills.

Health Awareness

Comprehensive health awareness campaigns covering nutrition, fitness, mental health, and disease prevention.

ICT & AI Training

Digital literacy and artificial intelligence training programs to prepare youth for the modern economy.

Environmental Conservation

Tree planting, waste management, and climate action programs led by youth participants.

Microenterprise Development

Youth entrepreneurship programs and support for small business development initiatives.

Community Outreach

Corporate social responsibility events and community engagement activities to build social cohesion.
